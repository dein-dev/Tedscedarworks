01 Ecology and zero waste
Our ambition is zero waste production. Therefore, we have minimized waste generated during raw material processing, recycling it into products such as pellets and animal bedding. All of this is done out of respect for wood, considering dwindling natural resources, and with a commitment to the future generations.

02 Passion and innovation
We are well acquainted with the industry and understand the challenges of sourcing high-quality straws. We decided to create a product initially dedicated to our horses, and now we want to share it with you as well. Our animal bedding is an ecological and hypoallergenic product born out of a passion for breeding and a commitment to the well-being of animals.

03 Green energy
We support environmental conservation and promote eco-friendly solutions. In our production, we prioritize ecology and sustainable development. Our company harnesses solar energy through our photovoltaic farm, aligning our operations with nature. By delivering high-quality products and ensuring harmony with the environment, we are committed to securing the future of our planet.
