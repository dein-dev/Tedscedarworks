Our projects are not just about production

We combine our passion for wood with a love for architecture and design. We design wooden buildings, work on interior concepts, and create designs for functional objects.
Wooden house projects
We bring to life projects of wooden houses. We 'll help create your dream space. Follow us to stay updated on new projects and offers that we prepare especially for you!
CAD/CAM Projects
We use the TopSolid software to design furniture and other products, customize design concepts, create prototypes, and program production processes, especially CNC machining. In conjunction with other tools, we transform designs into physical products.
Interior design projects
Through collaboration with architects, we offer the creation of an interior design project tailored to your preferences. Take advantage of our services to bring to life the interior you 've always dreamed of.
